HIGH-LEVEL TASK GOAL

As entered in Studio:

The goal of the task is to diagnose and fix a stall in which this mail service stops emitting new-mail events after several hours against a real IMAP account, recovers only on restart, and keeps its health endpoint green throughout. That last part is a symptom in its own right: the health check is not observing whatever has actually stopped. The investigation needs to reproduce the issue against a real account plus logs and connection state captured from the point where events stop. Success means the stall is reproduced and the state of the IMAP connections and the event pipeline at that point is established with evidence, explain the root cause, recovery becomes automatic so events resume without a restart, and the health signal reflects the stalled state instead of staying green. Restarting on a timer, or reconnecting on a fixed schedule without establishing the cause, is not the fix.
